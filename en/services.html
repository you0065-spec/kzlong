Embedded software and firmware for MCU / SoC platforms, driver porting and application logic — paired with schematic & PCB design to deliver production-ready mainboards or core boards.

  • · Cloud platform & app integration

Smart Appliance & Device Turnkey

For smart appliances, smart-home and consumer electronics — a full product solution from form-factor definition to software, hardware and cloud, balancing production stability and cost.

OEM / ODM & Mass Production Support
