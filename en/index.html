FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of clients do you serve?+
Brands, manufacturers and innovation teams that need smart-hardware products — from zero to launch or iterating an existing product. Start by telling us your requirements.
Can you handle only hardware, or also software and cloud?+
Either way. You can outsource a single layer or the full stack. For most products we recommend running software and hardware in parallel to avoid late-stage rework.
Can we validate a prototype before committing to volume?+
Yes. We typically start with a solution and prototype, verify function, reliability and cost, and only then move to mass production.
How are pricing and lead times determined?+
They depend on feature complexity, component selection and target volume. Send us your requirements and we will come back with a realistic assessment within one business day.
