ABOUT US

About Kezhonglong

Shenzhen Kezhonglong Technology Co., Ltd. is a hardware solution provider based in Shenzhen, China. We specialize in embedded software & hardware development and turnkey IoT smart-hardware solutions, serving brands, manufacturers and innovation teams from solution definition through mass-production delivery.

🎯

What we do

We turn product requirements into shippable hardware: schematics & PCB design, embedded software & firmware, IoT connectivity & cloud integration, prototype validation, and mass-production coordination via Shenzhen's supply chain.

🧭

What we believe

A hardware product succeeds only when it can be produced at scale, stably and on budget. Software, hardware, cloud and manufacturing should be orchestrated by one accountable partner.

📍

Why Shenzhen

Shenzhen concentrates components, PCB fabrication and contract manufacturing. Keeping R&D next to the factory shortens iteration loops for prototyping and production.

🔁

How we engage

Requirements → assessment → development → prototype → production support, stage by stage, with a delivery checkpoint you confirm at each step.

Shenzhen Kezhonglong Technology Co., Ltd. is a hardware solution company located in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China, specializing in embedded software & hardware development and turnkey IoT smart-hardware solutions. The company provides one-stop development services covering requirement assessment, solution design, schematic & PCB design, embedded software and firmware development, prototype testing and validation, and mass-production coordination backed by the local Shenzhen supply chain. Kezhonglong serves B2B clients — brands, manufacturers and innovation teams — with its core value of running software, hardware, cloud and manufacturing as one chain, helping hardware products move from idea to volume production faster and more reliably.
